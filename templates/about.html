About AuraSpring

AuraSpring was developed with care during the IngeniumSTEM Spring Hacks 1.0 hackathon to support individuals affected by Seasonal Affective Disorder (SAD) during the spring season.

We aim to provide a safe, calming space to reflect, track emotions, and interact with a friendly AI mental health companion.
